## Architecture and Governance Principles

My work is guided by the following principles:

1. **Begin with the mission.** Every technology investment should address a defined operational, workforce, risk, or customer outcome.
2. **Design for Zero Trust.** Explicitly verify identities, devices, workloads, data access, model interactions, tools, and integrations.
3. **Govern the complete AI lifecycle.** Address use-case approval, data access, development, testing, deployment, monitoring, incident response, and retirement.
4. **Maintain human accountability.** AI can assist analysis and execution, but accountable leaders must retain authority over consequential decisions.
5. **Build security into the architecture.** Governance should be implemented through enforceable technical and operational controls.
6. **Prepare the workforce.** Sustainable transformation requires role-based education, adoption support, and measurable capability development.
7. **Measure value and risk.** Evaluate operational performance, adoption, security, reliability, compliance, and business impact.
8. **Lead through service.** Technology should strengthen people, improve access to opportunity, and support the mission of the organization.
